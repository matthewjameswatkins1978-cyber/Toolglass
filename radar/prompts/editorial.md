# Toolglass Radar editorial brief

Find software a technically literate reader will be pleased to know about. Prefer mechanism, evidence, consequence and judgement over hype. Preserve uncertainty. Say clearly what the project claims, what public evidence shows, what Toolglass infers, and what Toolglass actually tested. Never imply hands-on use without hands-on evidence. Radar may publish nothing; reject mediocre candidates rather than filling a quota.
