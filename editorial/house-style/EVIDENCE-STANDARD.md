# Toolglass evidence standard

Evidence labels describe what Toolglass actually did. They are not scores, endorsements, or guarantees.

Evidence ladder:

`DISCOVERED` → `SCOUTED` → `INSPECTED` → `INSTALLED` → `TESTED` → `STRESS TESTED` → `MULTI-AGENT VERIFIED`

Use these supporting descriptions when needed:

- `VERIFIED` — directly supported by a recorded check or result.
- `HUMAN-OBSERVED` — observed by a person during a stated workflow.
- `PROJECT CLAIM` — stated by the upstream project and not independently established.
- `INFERENCE` — a reasoned interpretation, marked as such.
- `OPINION` — Toolglass judgement.

Article type and evidence status are separate. For example:

`article_type = Fresh Signal`  
`evidence_status = INSPECTED + FOCUSED TESTS`

Core rule:

> AI may expand evidence. It may not invent evidence.

If a test is bounded, name the boundary. Do not generalise a fixture, one platform, or one version into a universal claim. Keep private transcripts, credentials, raw history, and private paths out of public editorial files.
